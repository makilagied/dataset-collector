## What we are asking

You manage the Instagram professional account **@[account]**. We ask your permission to
collect the comments that other people post on your account's posts, using Instagram's
official API. We will use them to build a research dataset of abusive language in Swahili.

## What we collect

- The text of comments and replies on your posts, when they were posted, their like count,
  and whether they are hidden.
- Basic information about your posts: caption, type, link and date.
- Your account's username and Instagram account ID, so that we can link your data to this
  form.

## What we do not collect

- Commenters' usernames or account IDs. Each commenter is replaced by an irreversible code
  at the moment of collection.
- Private messages, your followers list, or anything from accounts other than yours.

## How the data is used and protected

- Comments are analysed automatically. A sample is labelled by trained annotators, who
  never see commenters' usernames: `@mentions` and links are masked.
- Your access token is stored encrypted. Data is kept on [location / provider], and only
  the research team can access it.
- Results are published only as aggregate statistics or anonymized examples.
